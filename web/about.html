About and limits

ESP means electric submersible pump. This page explains the score and its limits.

What the score means

The saved model scores this day’s readings for same-day running or failed status. The page shows its model score on a 0 to 100 scale and a Low, Elevated, or High band. The score is not a calibrated field probability and does not predict future failure.

Dataset limits

  • The project has no verified record of the dataset source.
  • The dataset covers 6 wells.
  • Tests on different wells produce results near chance.

Reading ranges

An out-of-range note compares a reading with the minimum and maximum values in the saved model’s training data. These ranges describe the data used to fit the model; they are not equipment limits.
